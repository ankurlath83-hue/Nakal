Nakal privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Nakal is a bill entry app for a business and its staff. This page says what the app stores and who can see it.

What the app stores

  • Sign-in details: the email address and password of each person who creates an account. Passwords are kept by the sign-in service in protected form; the app cannot read them.
  • Bills you enter: party name, date, item names, rates, quantities, transport charge and totals.

The app does not ask for or collect your location, contacts, photos, phone number, device identifiers or payment details.

If you turn on the PIN lock, the PIN is kept only on that device in scrambled form; it is never sent anywhere.

Where it is kept

Sign-in details and bills are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in a project managed by the app owner. Data travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. A copy of the bills is also kept on your device so the app can open without internet.

Who can see it

Only the owner and the staff emails the owner has approved can read or change bills, and they share the same bills. Creating an account does not by itself give access to any bills.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with other companies.

Deleting your data

Approved users can delete a bill inside the app. To have your account or all stored bills removed, ask the app owner using the contact below; it is done within 30 days.

Children

Nakal is a business tool and is not meant for children.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/ankurlath83-hue/nakal/issues.
