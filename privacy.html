Nakal privacy policy

Nakal is a bills and party ledger app for shops.

What is stored

Your bills, payments, parties and items are stored on your device, encrypted with a key made from your PIN. If you turn on online sync, the same records are encrypted on your device with your business key and then stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, Mumbai region). The server cannot read their contents.

What the server can see

If you sign in: your email address, the email addresses of staff you add, the number of records and the times they change.

What is not collected

No ads, no trackers, no analytics. No contacts, location, camera or microphone access. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

More > Backup and restore > Erase everything removes the book from the device. To delete an online account and its data, write to the developer using the contact shown on the app's Play Store page.
